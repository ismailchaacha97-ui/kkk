How to buy

Four minutes if you've never touched a wallet before. Base is an Ethereum L2, so fees are cents.

  1. Get a walletInstall Coinbase Wallet, Rabby or MetaMask. Write the seed phrase on paper. Never type it into anything, ever, including a site that looks exactly like this one.
  2. Put ETH on BaseBuy ETH anywhere, then withdraw it choosing the Base network. Coinbase does this with no bridge fee. Keep a couple of dollars for gas.
  3. Open Uniswap on BaseGo to app.uniswap.org, connect the wallet, switch the network to Base, and paste the contract address above into the token field.
  4. Swap and holdSet slippage to 1–3%, swap ETH for COO, confirm. The token appears in your wallet. There is nothing else to do. That is the whole product.

Why this isn't a rug

Not a promise — properties of the deployed bytecode that you can verify yourself on Basescan in thirty seconds.

✓ No mint function

The string mint does not appear in the public ABI. Supply is capped by the code, not by a pinky swear.

✓ Zero tax, no fee code

There is no fee logic anywhere in the contract, so there is no hidden dial to turn up to 99% after launch.

✓ No blacklist or pause

Nobody can freeze your balance or block your sell. The honeypot test is in the repo's test suite.

✓ Not upgradeable

No proxy, no delegatecall. What you verify today runs unchanged forever.

✓ Limits self-destruct

The 2% anti-sniper cap can never be lowered, never go under 1%, and expires automatically 24h after deploy.

✓ Ownership renounced

owner() returns the zero address. There are no admin keys left to steal or subpoena.

FAQ

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a picture of a bird attached to a number. Meme coins routinely go to zero, and this one very likely will. Spend only what you would happily set on fire.

Who is Sir Reginald Coo III?

A pigeon. He has no team, no VCs, no roadmap beyond eating. He was knighted by nobody and answers to no one.

Can the developer dump on me?

80% of supply is in the locked liquidity pool and ownership is renounced. The team wallet holds 5% and is published before launch — watch it on Basescan.

Why Base and not Solana or Ethereum?

Ethereum mainnet gas would cost more than the whole launch. Base is an Ethereum L2 with cent-level fees, real liquidity, and a Coinbase on-ramp your relatives can actually use.

Where's the audit?

There isn't one — a formal audit costs thousands. Instead the contract is ~120 lines, verified on Basescan, and the full test suite (including honeypot checks) is public on GitHub. Read it yourself; it's shorter than this FAQ.
