The 5 Core Pillars of the Strategy

Every trade signal in the MT4 indicator must satisfy this strict 5-layer confluence checklist before executing.

1

Static Support & Resistance Mapping

Marks horizontal support and resistance lines where price previously bounced or reversed aggressively. These key levels act as primary trigger zones — setups are analyzed when price approaches these zones.

2

Candlestick Rejection & Absorption

Executes strictly on completed candle closes (no premature limit guessing). Looks for long rejection wicks (pin bars ≥ 35%) and strong closing bodies. A long lower wick bouncing off support proves sellers were absorbed and buyers took total control.

3

Second Entry Failure Patterns (2L & 2S)

2L (Bullish): Pullback in uptrend → sellers try once (1st entry) → fail → sellers try a 2nd time → fail again. As price breaks higher, enter Long.
2S (Bearish): Pullback in downtrend → buyers attempt two rallies → both fail. Enter Short on breakdown.

4

Market Structure Alignment

Monitors structural swing points: Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL) for uptrends; Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL) for downtrends. Suppresses signals in choppy, sideways consolidations.

5

Confluence with the 13 / 50 EMA Dynamic Value Zone

Combines candlestick rejections and second-entry patterns with the 13 EMA (Dynamic Trigger) and 50 EMA (Macro Baseline Trend). A trade is only valid when the price action rejection happens right as price touches or tests the dynamic EMA line in confluence with static S/R.
