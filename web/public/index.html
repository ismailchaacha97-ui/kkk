Institutional Second Entry Price Action System

The Second Entry method (popularized by Al Brooks and Mack's Price Action Trading System - PATs) is based on a foundational market reality: counter-trend market participants almost always try twice to turn a strong trend, and when the second attempt fails, trapped traders fuel a rapid resumption of the major trend.

Second Entry Long (2EL) Setup

BULLISH
  1. 1
    Trend Established: Price is trading above the 21 EMA, and the EMA slope is angled upward.
  2. 2
    Leg 1 Pullback & 1EL: Price makes a swing high, then pulls back downward. A candle ticks above the prior candle's high, making the First Entry Long (1EL).
  3. 3
    Leg 2 Push Down (Trapping Bears): 1EL fails to create a new high; sellers push price lower a second time directly into the 21 EMA or a Key Naked Support Level.
  4. 4
    Signal Candle Confirmation: The candle produces a strong lower rejection wick (≥ 35% of bar range) bouncing sharply off the EMA/Support, closing in the upper half of the bar.
  5. ✓
    Execution: Enter Long on bar close or 1 tick above signal bar high. Stop loss placed 2 pips below signal bar low. TP at 1:1.5 to 1:2 R:R.

Second Entry Short (2ES) Setup

BEARISH
  1. 1
    Trend Established: Price is trading below the 21 EMA, and the EMA slope is angled downward.
  2. 2
    Leg 1 Pullback & 1ES: Price makes a swing low, then pulls back upward. A candle ticks below the prior candle's low, making the First Entry Short (1ES).
  3. 3
    Leg 2 Push Up (Trapping Bulls): 1ES fails to create a new low; buyers push price higher a second time directly into the 21 EMA or a Key Naked Resistance Level.
  4. 4
    Signal Candle Confirmation: The candle produces a strong upper rejection wick (≥ 35% of bar range) bouncing sharply off the EMA/Resistance, closing in the lower half of the bar.
  5. ✓
    Execution: Enter Short on bar close or 1 tick below signal bar low. Stop loss placed 2 pips above signal bar high. TP at 1:1.5 to 1:2 R:R.

Key Principles for Maximum Edge:

1. Context Over Signal

Never take a 2nd entry in a sideways chop or when price is slicing back and forth through the EMA without clean slope.

2. S/R Confluence

When the 21 EMA aligns directly with a naked horizontal support/resistance level, the bounce rate increases drastically.

3. Signal Candle Quality

The signal bar must show decisive rejection. Avoid dojis with wicks on both sides; prioritize clean pin bars closing in trend direction.
