## YOUR TRAINING ADJUSTED

You are not a beginner on weight, but 74kg at 175cm means you can handle volume. Stick to the 6-day split in PROGRAM.md.

**Your priority exercises for V-taper:**
1. Pull-ups - You MUST get to 12 clean. If you do <5 now, do 5x negatives (4 sec lower) daily. This alone = 50% of model look.
2. Lateral Raises - 4x15-20 twice/week. Shoulders = width.
3. Weighted Pushups - Add backpack 10-15kg. Chest line.
4. Bulgarian Split Squats - Don't skip. Model physique with chicken legs = stupid.

**For YOUR weight:**
- Pushups: Once you can do 20 clean, ALWAYS add 10-15kg backpack. Your bodyweight alone is not enough stimulus anymore at 74kg.
- Rows/Curls: Need at least one heavy band (30kg tension) or DB 12-16kg per hand. If you only have light bands/jugs, do 1.5x reps + slow 4 sec negative.
- Legs: Bodyweight squats are too easy for you. Must do single-leg work (Bulgarian, pistol progression, shrimp squat). Add backpack.

**Your conditioning:**
At 74kg targeting cut: **10k steps daily + 2x HIIT (from PROGRAM.md)**. No more. Extra cardio will just make you hungry and kill legs.
