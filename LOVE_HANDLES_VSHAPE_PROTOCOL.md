## 4. TRAINING - V vs BELLY

Your old training probably was cardio. Now switch:

**For belly/love handles:** Deficit + steps + core. **NOT** 100 side crunches (that builds oblique = WIDER waist). Do this instead:

- **Core 3x/week only, from PROGRAM.md:** Hanging leg raises + hollow holds. No weighted side bends.
- **Vacuum exercise (for transverse abs - pulls waist in):** Every morning 3x30sec: exhale fully, pull belly button to spine, hold. Bodybuilders use this to shrink waist 2-3cm over weeks.

**For V-shape:** This is your job now.

1.  **Pull-ups = #1 priority.** Wide lats make waist look -5cm instantly. Goal: 10-12 clean wide pull-ups.
    - If you do 0-4 now: 5 sets, do MAX reps + then 3 negatives (jump up, lower 4 sec). Do this 2x/week. In 6 weeks you'll double.
    - Add **Band Straight-Arm Pulldown** 3x15 to learn lat feeling.

2.  **Lateral Raises = #2.** Side delts = shoulder width. Do 4x15-20 TWICE a week (PUSH A & B). No cheating. Light weight, perfect form. Your shoulders will be sore = they grow.

3.  **Chest:** Weighted pushups 5x8-12 with backpack 10-15kg. Upper chest (feet elevated) = fills loose chest skin.

4.  **Rows:** 4x10-12 single-arm backpack rows to thicken back - V needs thickness, not just width.

**What to SKIP:** Heavy oblique work, long runs, ab machines.

Your **6-day split from PROGRAM.md is perfect.** Just emphasize those 4 above. Rest 90-120sec compounds.
