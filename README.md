## 3D Office Terminal

`office-terminal/` — a live 3D trading floor that runs on your own PC: real public market
prices, up to 10 autonomous day-traders with free will, memories and evolution, who argue
before every trade.

- **New to this? Read `office-terminal/START-HERE.md`** (plain-English, click-by-click).
- Windows: double-click `office-terminal/start.bat`
- macOS: double-click `office-terminal/start.command`
- Linux: run `office-terminal/start.sh`
- Technical details: `office-terminal/README.md`
