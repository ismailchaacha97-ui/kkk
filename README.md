# The Courage to Say Hello

*An honest book about talking to the girl you like — and becoming the kind of man worth talking to.*

**Read the full book: [THE-COURAGE-TO-SAY-HELLO.md](THE-COURAGE-TO-SAY-HELLO.md)** (~20,000 words, 19 chapters, 5 appendices)

Not a book of tricks. No manipulation, no "techniques," no lines. It rests on one idea: *a woman is not a puzzle to be solved, she is a person to be met* — and on the belief that warmth, clarity, humor and courage are learnable skills.

## Structure

| Part | Covers |
|---|---|
| **One — Before You Speak** | Fear, confidence, knowing what you actually feel, becoming worth talking to, reading context |
| **Two — The First Conversations** | Openers, listening, having something to say, humor and teasing, texting, flirting |
| **Three — From Talking to Something Real** | Asking her out, rejection, first dates, reading signals honestly |
| **Four — The Long Road** | Heartbreak, jealousy, respect and consent, the long game |
| **Appendices** | Field guide · 50 questions · mistakes diagnosed · a 90-day plan · further reading |

Every chapter ends with **The Practice** — one or two things to actually do.
