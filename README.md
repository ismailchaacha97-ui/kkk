## 🚀 Key Features

* **Classical Trend & Stage Engine**: 200 EMA (Wall Street Baseline) + 50 EMA + 20 EMA triple alignment with Stan Weinstein Stage Analysis.
* **Floor Trader Pivots (Daily PP, R1, R2, S1, S2)**: The exact mathematical support & resistance formulas used on Chicago and NY trading pits for over 40 years.
* **Momentum & Pullback Confluence**: RSI(14) + Williams %R(14) + Value Zone Pullback detection.
* **100% Non-Repainting Signals**: Formed and confirmed strictly on completed bars (Shift 1).
* **Bloomberg-Style On-Chart HUD Dashboard**:
  * Multi-Timeframe Alignment Matrix (M15, H1, H4, D1).
  * Confluence Score (+100% Bullish to -100% Bearish).
  * Market Regime & Stage Classifier.
  * Exact Trade Recommendation: Entry Price, Dynamic SL (pips & price), TP1 (1:1.5 RR), TP2 (1:2.5 RR).
  * Built-in Dynamic Lot Size Calculator (based on custom % account balance risk).
* **Multi-Channel Alerts**: Sound, Pop-up alert, Push notification to MT4 Mobile App, and Email alert.
