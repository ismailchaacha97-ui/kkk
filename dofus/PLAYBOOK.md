# DOFUS — ZERO TO STANDOUT

### A complete field manual for a returning player who knows nothing

*Written for Dofus 3 (Unity client), mono-account, starting from scratch — with a real income plan attached.*

---

## 0. Read this before you create a character

You played this as a kid. That means you have **nostalgia, not knowledge**. Nearly everything you remember is wrong: spells have been rewritten, idols were deleted from the game, prospecting was gutted, the client is now Unity, and leveling is quest-driven rather than mob-farming-driven.

So here is the deal this document makes with you:

| Phase | Duration | Goal | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Start** | Weeks 1–4 | Learn the game, reach ~level 100 | You understand stats, the HDV, and your class |
| **Build** | Weeks 5–12 | Reach ~level 200, bank 5–20M kamas | You have a character that can actually earn |
| **Standout** | Month 4+ | Turn knowledge into income | 1,000–3,000 MAD/month and a name people know |

**The single most important rule:** do not chase money in month one. You cannot sell kamas you don't have, and you cannot farm profitably on a character that dies to Astrub mobs. The money is a *consequence* of getting good. Everyone who tries to skip this step ends up with a level 60 character, 200k kamas, and a Discord full of scammers.

# PART 1 — SETUP

## 1.1 Server choice

Play a **single-account (mono) server** — Draconiros or whichever mono server you're placed on.

| | Mono-account | Multi-account |
|---|---|---|
| Kama value | **Higher** (e.g. ~€0.90/M) | Lower (e.g. ~€0.46/M) |
| Economy | Player-driven, healthy, social | Flooded with farmed supply |
| Your experience | Real groups, real reputation | You play alone, 4 windows |
| Best for | **You** | Established farmers with capital |

Mono servers are where your time is worth the most and where a reputation is actually worth building. Multi-account servers are a volume game you cannot win as a beginner.

## 1.2 Class choice

This is the only irreversible-feeling decision, and it's less important than the internet suggests — you get an XP bonus for having a higher-level character, so your second character levels faster. Pick once, commit, move on.

| Class | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Cra** | ⭐ **Best first pick** | Huge range, so you learn monster spell ranges safely. Strong at every level, welcome in groups, forgiving of mistakes. Agility to ~150, then Intelligence. |
| **Sacrier** | ⭐ **Best if you want speed** | Strongest sub-200 class in the game: damage, healing, AoE, tanky. Agility build. Melds into melee, so fights are fast. Weak at 200. |
| **Feca** | Solid alternative | Tanky, forgiving, flexible elements. Slower, less aggressive. |
| **Enutrof** | Good, slightly advanced | Ranged with utility and team buffs. Chance build 1–200. |
| **Pandawa** | Great endgame, bad start | Weakest leveling class, most demanded class at 200. Not a first character. |
| **Rogue / Xelor / Eliotrope** | ❌ Avoid | Genuinely hard, weak before 200, expensive at 200. |
| **Ecaflip / Masqueraider** | ❌ Skip | Jack of all trades, weak at everything. |

**My recommendation for you: Cra.** You said you know nothing. Cra is the class that teaches you the game instead of punishing you for not knowing it. If you want to go faster and don't mind melee, take Sacrier.

## 1.3 Subscription

- **Free**: Incarnam, Astrub and surroundings. Enough to decide if you're hooked.
- **Paid**: **€5.99/month** (cheaper on 3/6/12-month plans). Unlocks the whole world, professions past 30, and faction PvP.

Budget it as a business cost, because it is one: €5.99 ≈ **6.7M kamas at retail** at mono-server prices, so you need to be earning meaningfully more than 7M/month before this is "free."

## 1.4 First-hour settings checklist

- [ ] Complete the **Envoy of the Twelve** intro quest — it teaches combat and gives starter gear
- [ ] Turn on **auto-follow group leader** and **auto-join fights** (huge quality-of-life in Unity)
- [ ] Bind your spells to keys 1–8 and learn them by muscle memory, not by looking
- [ ] Set fight speed to maximum
- [ ] Join a **guild** before level 50 — group content, free help, and your first social network
- [ ] Open the **Achievements** tab and read it. This is now your quest log, your XP source, and your kama source

# PART 2 — THE MECHANICS THAT MATTER

Most players never learn these five things. They are the entire difference between a character that works and one that struggles.

## 2.1 Characteristic points

You get **5 points per level**, for **995 total at level 200**. Cost per point is not flat:

| Stat | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Vitality** | 1/1 | HP. Cheap and always correct. |
| **Element** (Str/Int/Cha/Agi) | 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 as it climbs | Damage in that element |
| **Wisdom** | 3/1 | ≈ **+1% XP per point**, plus AP/MP loss resistance |

**The rule:** one main element, then dump spare points into Vitality. Wisdom is a choice — extremely good while leveling, but it costs 3 points per 1, so it makes you fragile. New players should mostly ignore it; veterans stack it on dedicated leveling gear.

## 2.2 Wisdom is the leveling stat

Wisdom raises XP from every fight. In Dofus 3 the **idols were removed**, so the old trick of stacking idol multipliers is gone — Wisdom now comes from your stats and your gear. This is why leveling gear often looks "bad" (low damage) but levels you faster.

## 2.3 Prospecting = drops

Prospecting (PP) improves your drop rate. Every **10 Chance = 1 Prospecting**. Base is 100 for everyone (120 for Enutrof). Prospecting was **nerfed hard** in the run-up to Dofus 3 to reduce resource inflation — so don't build your whole income around PP stacking like players did in 2013.

## 2.4 AP and MP breakpoints — the most important gear concept

Your damage comes from how many spells you cast per turn. So the only gear questions that matter early are:

- **Do I reach 8 AP?** (one more spell per turn = ~30% more damage)
- **Do I reach 10 AP?**
- **Can I get +1 MP?** (positioning = surviving = damage)

A cheap set that hits 8 AP beats an expensive set that doesn't. **Never buy an item for raw damage if it breaks your AP/MP breakpoint.** Every gear decision in Part 4 comes back to this.

## 2.5 Anomaly zones — free XP multiplier

Zones rotate a **time-limited bonus** that peaks at **120%**, and at maximum it gives an XP multiplier around **2.2×**.

- Open the map, filter territories by bonus.
- Find a zone near your level with a high bonus.
- Grind quick fights there, not long ones. Chaining fast kills beats one long fight every time.
- **Almanax** also grants daily bonuses (+50% to +150% XP in specific zones/dungeons). Check it every day; it's a free multiplier most players ignore.

# PART 3 — THE LEVEL ROUTE, 1 → 200

Follow **quests and achievements**, not mob grinding. Achievements give one-time XP, kamas, and permanent bonuses — and running the main questline beats killing the same monsters forever.

| Levels | Where | What to do | Gear goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1–20** | Incarnam | ~25 quests, exploration achievements, **Kardorim dungeon** | Intrépide / Boune set, Piwi drops |
| **20–40** | Astrub | Full Astrub questline + achievements. Return to Rathrosk for the **Silver Dofus** | Tofu/Toady (Agi), Mousse (+1 AP), Bouftou (+1 AP) |
| **40–60** | Amakna | **Blop dungeons** (4 of them), Crackler dungeon | Add a **Gelano** for +1 AP |
| **60–85** | Wabbit Island | Quests + achievements → **Cawwot Dofus** | Stabilise your AP |
| **80–100** | Moon Island | Achievements → **Dokoko Dofus**; join a guild for dungeons | Royal Blop sets (AP/MP bonus at 3–4 pieces) |
| **100–130** | Frigost, Otomai, Pandala | **Emerald Dofus** questline; class Dopple daily for Doploons | First real set upgrade |
| **130–150** | Brakmar/Bonta, dimensions | **Crimson Dofus**, **Watchers Dofus**, Ecaflipus/Srambad/Xelorium dimensions | Mid-tier set |
| **150–180** | Frigost 2 & 3, Otomai dungeons | Dungeons your group clears fast | Minotot / Moowolf / Soft Oak / Treadfast sets |
| **180–200** | Dimensions, Frigost 3 | Anomaly grinding + dungeon achievements | Endgame set, then **mage it** |

**Gear milestone levels: 50, 80, 100, 130, 150, 180.** Plan upgrades at those numbers instead of buying impulsively.

⚠️ **Two quests need a profession at level 50.** If you skip professions, you will hit a wall on the Emerald Dofus line. Start one profession by level 40 — not level 60.

## 3.1 Dofus list — these are the real progression markers

Silver → Cawwot → Dokoko → Emerald → Crimson → Turquoise → Watchers → Ivory/Ebony/Ochre.

The first four are achievable by a solo player and give large permanent stat bonuses. Don't think of them as optional collectibles; they *are* the progression system.

## 3.2 Realistic timelines

| Playtime | Time to 200 |
|---|---|
| 1–2 h/day, casual, doing quests | **4–12 months** |
| 3–4 h/day, following an optimised route | **4–8 weeks** |
| Paid leeching | days — but costs the kamas you don't have yet |

Anyone promising "200 in a week, free, no leech" is selling you something. Ignore them.

# PART 4 — GEAR: HOW TO NOT WASTE KAMAS

Ninety percent of new players lose their money on gear. Here is the discipline.

## 4.1 The four rules

1. **Never break an AP/MP breakpoint to gain stats.** 8 AP → 9 AP is worth more than 80 points of Strength.
2. **Buy at set bonuses.** Sets give AP/MP at 3–4 pieces. Buying 2 pieces of a set is often worse than 4 pieces of a cheaper one.
3. **One element at a time.** Splitting into two elements before level 150 halves your damage.
4. **Don't mage anything below level 180.** Forgemagery is where rich players print money and where new players incinerate it.

## 4.2 The 30% rule

Never spend more than **30% of your kamas** on a single gear upgrade, no matter how good it looks. The other 70% is your working capital. You will need it at 100, at 150, and at 200 — and the players who go broke are always the ones who spent everything at 80.

## 4.3 Cheap beats expensive, always

Use a gear optimiser (Dofus Fashionista and similar sites) and set a **budget constraint**. You will routinely find that a 300k item outperforms a 3M item for your specific build because it hits the AP breakpoint. The HDV is full of overpriced items whose price is a function of prestige, not function.

# PART 5 — THE MONEY ENGINE

**This is the part that eventually becomes the business.** Start it at level 1, not level 200.

## 5.1 What beginners actually earn

| Stage | Realistic daily income |
|---|---|
| Fresh account, level 1–60 | 50,000 – 200,000 kamas/day |
| Level 100+, decent gear | 300,000 – 1,000,000 kamas/day |
| Level 200, skilled, multiple methods | 500,000 – 5,000,000 kamas/day |

At mono-server retail (~€0.90/M), a good level-200 player is generating roughly **€0.45–4.50/day** of theoretical value. Let that number sit with you. It's why Part 6 exists, and why "sell kamas" is not the plan.

## 5.2 The four income skills, in order of accessibility

**① Gathering professions — zero capital required.** The most reliable beginner income by far. Harvest, sell raw materials on the HDV. No investment, no risk, and it teaches you the market.

**② Achievements and dungeon quests.** One-time XP *plus* kamas *plus* permanent bonuses. Players who "grind monsters" earn half what players who clear achievements earn.

**③ HDV flipping (arbitrage).** Buy low, sell high. Start small, at level 60, with resources — not equipment. This is the skill that eventually scales into everything.

**④ Crushing and forging (brisage/FM).** Buy cheap equipment, crush it into runes, sell runes. High margin, high knowledge requirement. Level 150+.

## 5.3 Which professions to pick

| Pair | Why |
|---|---|
| **Farmer + Baker** | Cheapest to level, bread always sells, materials self-supplied |
| **Miner + Jeweller** | Ores into jewellery, feeds crafting profitably |
| **Alchemist** | Potions are among the most traded items on any server |

Pick **Farmer** if you want the easiest start. Level it in 15–20 minute harvest sessions in the Astrub fields; wheat grows everywhere and always sells. You need level 50 in *some* profession anyway for the Dofus quests, so this is not a detour.

## 5.4 How to read the HDV like a trader

Once a week, spend 20 minutes doing this. It is the highest-return activity in the entire game for a beginner.

1. Open the HDV and pick **10 resources or items** you encounter while playing.
2. For each, record: **price, daily volume, and how fast it sells out**.
3. Look for the gap: high volume + stable price = reliable income. High price + low volume = dead inventory trap.
4. Note **time-of-day and weekday patterns**. Prices drop on weekends when everyone farms and rise midweek when everyone buys. The classic cycle: **buy Sunday night, sell Wednesday.**
5. Sell in **medium batches**, never one giant stack — one bulk listing crashes the price you get.

## 5.5 The bank rule

Every time you cross a round number of kamas, move **70% to the bank** and keep 30% liquid. The bank is your gear fund, your emergency fund, and your discipline. If you find yourself with 2M and no plan, bank 1.4M immediately.

# PART 6 — THE STANDOUT LAYER

Anyone can reach 200. Almost nobody does these six things. This is where you separate from 99% of players — and it's the only route from "player" to "income."

## 6.1 Keep a journal from day one

Screenshot HDV prices weekly. Note what you sold and for how much. Log your kamas daily, one line.

Ninety-nine percent of players who "played for a year and have nothing" never looked at the market once. If you log prices for three months, you'll know things about your server's economy that level-200 players don't. **This is your edge, and it costs nothing.**

## 6.2 Become the expert in one narrow thing

Not "Dofus player." Pick one: *the Cra gearing guy*, *the Emerald Dofus guy*, *the kamas-per-hour guy*, *the Arabic Dofus explainer*.

Breadth is worthless in a 20-year-old game. Narrow + deep is the entire competitive strategy. Pick a niche by month two and publish only that.

## 6.3 Publish in Arabic

This is the biggest open gap in the entire Dofus ecosystem and it is sitting right in front of you.

Dofus content is overwhelmingly French. The Arabic-speaking player base is large, underserved, and undertaught. You would be **competing against nobody** instead of against fifteen established French creators. Do Arabic voiceover with French and English subtitles — that gives you three audiences from one video.

## 6.4 Build the Discord early

A Discord is not a vanity project; it's distribution and supply. Arabic gaming Discords are the growth channel. By month three it's a place to sell services, recruit dungeon groups, and get your first buyers when the money phase starts.

## 6.5 The content flywheel

One video a day, ten days, then evaluate. The formats that work for Dofus:

- **"Road to 200" series** — episodic, cheap to make, naturally retentive
- **Weekly kamas report** — 5 minutes on what's spiking on the HDV. Repeatable forever, and it makes you the reference
- **Build/gear explainers** — evergreen, searched forever
- **Mistake videos** — "5 things I wasted kamas on." Highest engagement format in gaming

## 6.6 Reputation is the actual asset

In a game where every transaction between players carries scam risk, **being the person who doesn't scam** is worth real money. Answer questions, honour deals, don't beg, don't spam. It compounds for years.

# PART 8 — RULES, RISK, AND THE LINE

## 8.1 What Ankama actually forbids

Read this once, properly:

- Selling, buying, trading or **giving accounts** — forbidden
- Trading kamas **outside the official kama markets** — forbidden
- **Inter-server trading** — forbidden
- Botting, and any automation — forbidden

Sanctions escalate to **permanent bans of all linked accounts**. Advertising kama sales in-game is an automatic ban. Ban waves catch **buyers as well as sellers**, which is why the entire grey market runs on fear.

## 8.2 If you ever do sell anything, know your payment rails

You're in Morocco, and this is where people get hurt:

- **PayPal**: works to receive. Moroccan terms require withdrawal within **30 days**, and withdrawal runs through **CASHPLUS**. Chargebacks will eat you alive — one disputed payment wipes out days of profit.
- **Crypto**: **do not.** The 2017 Bank Al-Maghrib / Office des Changes notice still governs. Buying crypto abroad without authorisation is an FX infraction, with reported fines of **MAD 20,000–100,000** for individuals.
- **Tax**: service income is declarable from the first dirham. The **auto-entrepreneur** regime is free and taxes services at **1% of turnover** — the cheapest legal wrapper you have.

## 8.3 The honest math on selling kamas

| | Value |
|---|---|
| 1 hour of efficient solo farming | ~1M kamas |
| Retail price, mono server | ~€0.90 |
| **Your hourly rate** | **~9.6 MAD/hour** |
| Morocco minimum wage (SMIG 2026) | **17.92 MAD/hour** |

Farming kamas to sell pays less than half the legal minimum wage, before subscription, electricity, and ban risk. This is not a business. It is a hobby that pays for itself badly.

**What actually works:** selling *services* (zero capital, priced on the buyer's impatience) and *resale* (priced on liquidity and reputation) — both of which require you to be good at the game first. Which is why Part 3 exists.
