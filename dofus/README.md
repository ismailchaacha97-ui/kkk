## The short version

**Months 1–3: play, don't sell.** Cra (or Sacrier) on a mono server, €5.99/month, follow Astrub → Wabbit → Moon → Emerald → Crimson, start Farmer by level 40 (the Emerald questline requires profession 50 — this is the wall everyone hits), keep a weekly HDV price log, join a guild.

**Month 4 onward: monetise what you know.**
- Services (carries, leveling, coaching) → fastest, zero capital
- Resale (buy wholesale, sell retail) → 3,000 MAD/month at ~1,100M turnover, ~3,600 MAD of float
- Arabic-language content → zero capital, 12–24 months
- Farming kamas to sell → ~9.6 MAD/hour. Below half the Moroccan minimum wage.

**The edge nobody uses:** log HDV prices weekly for three months. It costs 20 minutes a week and it's the only thing that turns a player into someone who understands the economy.
