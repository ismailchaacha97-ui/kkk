**Verdict codes:** `FARM` = worth harvesting · `FLIP` = price swings, buy low · `DEAD` = low volume, avoid · `WATCH` = unclear, keep logging

### The patterns to look for

- **High volume + stable price** → reliable income. This is your bread and butter.
- **High price + low volume** → dead inventory trap. You'll list it and it'll sit for a month.
- **Weekend low / midweek high** → buy Sunday night, sell Wednesday.
- **Sudden spike** → an update, a new recipe, or a youtuber made a video. Find out why before you chase it.
