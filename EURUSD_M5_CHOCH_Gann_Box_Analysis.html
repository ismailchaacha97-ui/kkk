Step 0 — Definitions used

“Start of HIGH or LOW”

The origin pivot of the impulse leg that defines the current structure — the absolute swing extreme where the move that will be broken begins. For a bearish structure the start is a swing HIGH; for a bullish structure the start is a swing LOW. On this chart the active structure is bearish, so its start is the high at 15 Sep 01:50 ≈ 1.15530.

CHOCH (Change of Character)

The first counter-trend break of the most recent opposing pivot:

  • Bullish CHOCH — price is printing lower lows / lower highs; the first candle close above the last lower high (LH) flips the character to bullish.
  • Bearish CHOCH — mirror image: first close below the last higher low in an uptrend.
  • It differs from BOS (Break of Structure), which is a break in the direction of the trend (continuation), not against it.

Gann box as applied here

A rectangle anchored at the structure start ① and at the terminal point of the broken structure ② — the exact pivot from which the breaking impulse launches. Its horizontal grid divides the structure range into eighths; the 0.5 (50%) line is the bisector of the full ①→② range and is the entry trigger of this strategy.
