# NUTRITION FOR MODEL PHYSIQUE - AT HOME

Training is 30%. This is 70%.

### Step 1: Find Your Maintenance Calories

**Quick formula (no lab needed):**
- Bodyweight in kg x 33 = Maintenance (if active 5-6x/week + steps)
- Bodyweight in kg x 30 = Maintenance (if 3-4x/week)
- Example: 75kg x 33 = 2475 kcal/day

**More accurate (Mifflin):**
BMR = 10*weight(kg) + 6.25*height(cm) - 5*age + 5 (men) / -161 (women)
TDEE = BMR x 1.5 (if training 5-6x) or x1.35 (if 3-4x)

Track weight for 2 weeks eating normally. If weight stable, that's maintenance.

### Step 2: Pick Goal

**A) You have belly/love handles, no visible abs (15%+ BF for men, 24%+ for women):**
- CUT: Maintenance - 400 kcal
- Aim: -0.4 to -0.6kg/week
- Don't go below bodyweight(kg) x 22 kcal. Example: 75kg minimum ~1650 kcal.

**B) You're skinny/skinny-fat, light weight, no muscle (~12-15% BF but no size):**
- LEAN BULK: Maintenance + 200 kcal
- Aim: +0.2 to +0.3kg/week
- This builds chest/shoulders width.

**C) You're average (can see top abs faintly in morning):**
- RECOMP: Maintenance - 100 to +100 kcal (eat at maintenance, high protein)
- Works for beginners first 12 weeks.

### Step 3: Macros (Non-Negotiable)

1.  **Protein:** 1.8 - 2.2g per kg bodyweight. EVERY DAY. 75kg = 150-165g protein.
2.  **Fat:** 0.8 - 1.1g per kg. 75kg = 60-80g.
3.  **Carbs:** Rest of calories. Carbs fuel workouts. Don't fear them.

**Example for 75kg on CUT (2100 kcal):**
- Protein: 160g = 640 kcal
- Fat: 70g = 630 kcal
- Carbs: ~207g = 830 kcal

**Track with:** MyFitnessPal or Yazio (free). Weigh food for 2 weeks until you eyeball.

### Step 4: Meal Structure (Simple Moroccan/Home Friendly)

You don't need 6 meals, chicken & rice 24/7. 3-4 meals with protein each.

**Protein sources (pick 2-3 daily):**
- Eggs (6g per egg), Chicken breast/thigh, Sardines/tuna, Ground beef, Lentils + eggs, Whey (~24g/scoop), Greek yogurt/lben, Chickpeas

**Carbs:**
- Oats, Rice, Whole wheat bread/khobz, Potatoes, Pasta, Fruits (banana, apple), Dates

**Fats:**
- Olive oil, Almonds/peanuts, Avocado, Egg yolk, Cheese

**Sample Day - 2100 kcal CUT (adjust portions):**

- **Breakfast (8h):** 3 eggs + 80g oats + 1 banana + 10g peanut butter = ~550 kcal, 30g P
- **Lunch (13h):** 150g chicken + 200g rice + salad + 1 tbsp olive oil = ~600 kcal, 45g P
- **Pre-workout (16h):** 1 apple + 30g whey + water = ~200 kcal, 25g P
- **Dinner (19h30):** 150g ground beef or fish + 300g potatoes + veggies = ~550 kcal, 40g P
- **Snack (21h30):** 200g Greek yogurt or 250ml milk + 20g almonds = ~220 kcal, 18g P

**Total: ~2120 kcal, 158g P, 70g F, 210g C**

On LEAN BULK, add: +40g oats at breakfast + 1 extra khobz at lunch.

### Step 5: Rules

1.  **Protein every meal (40g+).** If you miss protein target, you lose muscle on cut / don't grow on bulk.
2.  **Water:** 2.5 - 3.5L / day. 500ml extra on training days.
3.  **Sleep:** 7.5-9h. No sleep = no fat loss, no testosterone, no recovery. Non-negotiable.
4.  **Alcohol:** Kills model look. If you drink: 1x/week max, 2 drinks max, count calories.
5.  **Sugar/Processed:** Limit to 10% of calories. One small treat/day max on cut.
6.  **Vegetables:** 400g+ daily (salad, tomatoes, carrots, broccoli). Fiber keeps you full.
7.  **Creatine (optional but recommended):** 5g/day, any brand monohydrate, no loading. Cheap, safe, +3-5% strength. Take anytime.
8.  **Weigh food raw.** Cooked weight lies.

### Step 6: Adjustments

Every 2 weeks:

- Weight not dropping on CUT? -150 to -200 kcal OR +2k steps/day. Don't do both.
- Strength dropping fast? You cut too hard. Add 150 kcal.
- Not gaining on BULK after 3 weeks? +150 kcal.
- Constant hunger? Eat more volume: potatoes > rice, 500g veggies, casein at night.

### Supplements - What Actually Works

- **Whey protein:** Only if you can't hit protein with food. Food > powder.
- **Creatine monohydrate:** Yes. 5g/day.
- **Vitamin D3 1000-2000IU:** If you stay indoors (Moroccan sun is enough if outside 20min daily).
- **Omega-3:** If you don't eat fish 2x/week.
- **Rest is scams:** Fat burners, BCAAs, mass gainers, testosterone boosters = waste of money.
