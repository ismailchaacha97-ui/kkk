## A Note Before You Begin

This is not a book of tricks.

I want to say that at the top, plainly, because the shelf this book sits on is crowded with liars. There is an entire industry built on the promise that women are a lock and that somewhere out there is a key — a phrase, a posture, a text-message formula, a "technique" — that will open her without her consent and without your growth.

That industry is selling you two things at once: a fantasy and a wound. The fantasy is that you can skip the hard part. The wound is the belief, planted quietly underneath, that who you actually are could never be enough, so you had better learn to perform someone else.

Both are false, and both will cost you years.

Here is what I believe instead, and what this whole book rests on:

**A woman is not a puzzle to be solved. She is a person to be met.**

Everything useful in these pages follows from that one sentence. If you understand it in your bones, you will not need most of the chapters. If you do not understand it yet, the chapters will help you get there.

That said — and this matters — "be yourself" is incomplete advice. It is true, and it is not enough. Being yourself while mumbling at the floor, while texting eleven times in a row, while never actually saying what you want, will not work, and it is not fair to call that a failure of the world. Skill exists. Timing exists. Warmth, humor, clarity, restraint — these are learnable. A man can be entirely genuine and entirely unskilled at once, and the fix is not to become fake. The fix is to become *fluent*.

This book is about fluency.

### Who this book is for

- The man who has liked someone for months and has said nothing.
- The man who says plenty and it never seems to land.
- The man who is fine in every room until *she* walks into it.
- The man who has been rejected once and has been treating it like a verdict.
- The man who is doing all right, honestly, but wants to stop leaving so much unsaid.

### Who this book is not for

- Anyone looking for manipulation, pressure, "negging," or anything that treats a woman's discomfort as a step in a process.
- Anyone hoping to make a specific person love them. That is not available. It has never been available, to anyone, ever. What is available is a real chance, honestly taken.

### How to read it

There are four parts.

**Part One — Before You Speak** is about you: your nerves, your story, your worth, and the difference between confidence and performance.

**Part Two — The First Conversations** is the practical core: opening, listening, talking, texting, humor, and the art of ending a conversation well.

**Part Three — From Talking to Something Real** covers asking her out, the first date, reading interest honestly, rejection, and the slow work of getting closer.

**Part Four — The Long Road** is about what you do after — conflict, jealousy, heartbreak, patience, and the man you're building either way.

At the end of every chapter there is a short section called **The Practice**, with one or two things to actually do. Reading this book will change nothing. Doing three of the practices will change more than reading it twice.

Read it in order the first time. After that, open it wherever you need it.

One last thing before Chapter One.

You are going to feel, at some point in these pages, a little exposed — like I am describing something you thought only you did. The rehearsing. The refreshing. The story you tell yourself about why you didn't go over.

I am describing it because I did it too. All of it. This book is not written down at you from some summit. It is written across a table.

Let's begin.
