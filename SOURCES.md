# Sources: Old Books + Papers

## Old trading books (the forgotten core)

- George D. Taylor — *The Taylor Trading Technique* (1950). 3-Day Cycle: Buy Day / Sell Day / Sell Short Day, Book Method, Buy Day Low Violation, penetration failures, rally number.
  - Overview: https://polaristradinggroup.com/glossary/taylor-trading-technique-a-k-a-3-day-cycle/
  - Primer: https://tradersmastermind.com/the-taylor-trading-technique/
  - Raschke modern translation (slides): https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/linda-bradford-raschke-the-taylor-trading-techniqueppt/262013129
  - Forum breakdown with cycle rules: https://www.forexfactory.com/thread/1343895-taylor-trading-journal

- Jesse Livermore — *How to Trade in Stocks* (1940) + Wyckoff interviews (*Jesse Livermore's Methods of Trading in Stocks*).
  - Pivotal points, 3-pt continuation / 6-pt reversal, 48-hour rule, pyramiding, time stops.
  - Summary: https://www.investopedia.com/articles/trading/09/legendary-trader-jesse-livermore.asp
  - Book background: https://books.google.com/books/about/Jesse_Livermore_s_Methods_of_Trading_in.html?id=gsA3LgEACAAJ

- Humphrey B. Neill — *Tape Reading and Market Tactics* (1931), *The Art of Contrary Thinking* (1954).
  - Tape = human nature; public wrong at turning points; price+volume; judgment > mechanics.
  - Bio/essay: https://capitalideasonline.com/wordpress/contrarian-thinking/

- Gerald M. Loeb — *The Battle for Investment Survival* (1935).
  - Ever-liquid account, concentrate in liquid leaders, cut losses fast, let winners run.
  - https://openlibrary.org/works/OL2987338W/The_battle_for_investment_survival

- W.D. Gann — *45 Years in Wall Street* (1949), *How to Make Profits Trading in Commodities*.
  - 24 never-failing rules / 28 rules: stops, breakeven fast, diversify, small size.
  - Rules discussion: https://www.investingsystems.com/3-trading-rules-from-a-book-written-in-1949/
  - Methodology sampler (PDF): https://ptgmedia.pearsoncmg.com/images/9780132734387/samplepages/0132734389.pdf

## Research papers (the proof)

- Cooper, Cliff & Gulen (2008) — *Return Differences between Trading and Non-Trading Hours: Like Night and Day.* Entire US premium overnight; intraday ~0/negative.
  - https://www.ssrn.com/abstract=1004081
  - https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1004081

- Lou, Polk, Skouras et al. — *A Tug of War: Overnight Versus Intraday Expected Returns.* Momentum/reversal 100% overnight, persists years; clientele effects.
  - https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2018/preliminary/paper/ds4hZdf8

- Hendershott et al. / Cross-section of intraday and overnight returns — arbitrageurs cut into close due to overnight risk/fees; mispricing worsens EOD, 4× Friday.
  - https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X21000854

- Lakonishok & Smidt (1988) + McConnell & Xu (2008) — *Equity Returns at the Turn of the Month.* All excess in 4 ToM days over 109 years (1897-2005).
  - https://docs.lib.purdue.edu/ciberwp/43/
  - https://www.chesler.us/resources/academia/turn_of_the_month_stock_returns.pdf

- Lucca & Moench (2015) — *The Pre-FOMC Announcement Drift.* ~80% of excess in 24h pre-FOMC 1994-2011.
  - https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228316087_The_Pre-FOMC_announcement_drift
  - Disappearing post-2015 (reduced uncertainty): https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1544612320315956
  - PDF: https://www.skidmore.edu/economics/documents/KurovWolfeGilbert-TheDisappearingPre-FOMC-Announce-Drift-200914.pdf

- Hu et al. (NBER) — *Premium for Heightened Uncertainty.* Pre-announcement drift before ALL macros (~25bps close-to-minutes-before), variance-uncertainty resolution.
  - https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w25817/revisions/w25817.rev1.pdf

- Heston & Sadka (2008) — Same-calendar-month seasonality persists 20 years; 50bps+/month.
  - Summary: https://www.rhsmith.umd.edu/news/seasonality-stock-market-returns
  - Extensions: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S092753981830077X
  - Seasonal reversals (mispricing proof): https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X20301951

- George & Hwang (2004) — *The 52-Week High and Momentum Investing.* Near-high beats past-returns momentum.
  - Bhootra & Hur (2013) — *Timing of 52-week high:* recency doubles it.
  - https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378426613002446
  - International evidence: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S106297690700021X

- Anomaly overviews (for context that most anomalies fade, these survive):
  - https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/119039/1/MPRA_paper_119039.pdf
  - https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165410124000181
